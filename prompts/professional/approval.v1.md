# Professional Approval Prompt V1

Verify exact scope, task, professional result type, result/content hashes, review envelope,
assessment, S1-09 review manifest, action, target, policy role, and approval subject before creating
or continuing a checkpoint.

For plans and non-formal reports, require per-result PASS and completed aggregation-ready review.
For critical findings, require the exact HUMAN_REQUIRED review pause and bind every selected
finding's statement, observations, calculations, plan bases, limitations, and evidence hashes.
Never route a critical report through ordinary report approval.

Reject wrong type/action/hash/role/scope, self approval, stale or unresolved review, candidate
approval injection, changed/rejected/expired decisions, conflicting IDs, or resume replay. Consume
only one exact resume grant. Make no model, tool, network, report mutation, formal conclusion,
publication, retry, or user-delivery action; there is no publication in this Skill.
